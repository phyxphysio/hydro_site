What to Expect

Your First Hydrotherapy Session

Here's what you can expect during your first visit:

Warm Welcome and Orientation

Our staff will greet you and show you around the facilities, including changing rooms and pool area.

Initial Assessment

Your physiotherapist will discuss your health goals and any specific concerns or limitations.

Guided Session

You'll be guided through gentle exercises in our warm water pool, tailored to your specific needs.

Post-Session Feedback

After your session, you'll have the opportunity to discuss how you felt and what adjustments might be needed.
